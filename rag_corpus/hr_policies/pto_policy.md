# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy
**Northlane Systems — Human Resources**
**Effective Date:** March 1, 2025 (revised January 2026)
**Document ID:** HR-POL-007

## Overview
Northlane Systems provides a flexible PTO program covering vacation, personal time, and short-term illness. This policy does not cover parental leave, bereavement leave, or long-term disability, which are addressed in separate policies (HR-POL-009, HR-POL-011).

## Accrual
Full-time employees accrue PTO at the following rates based on tenure:

| Tenure | Accrual Rate |
|---|---|
| 0–2 years | 15 days/year |
| 3–5 years | 20 days/year |
| 6+ years | 25 days/year |

PTO accrues monthly and is prorated for employees who start mid-year. Part-time employees accrue PTO on a pro-rata basis matching their scheduled hours.

## Requesting Time Off
Employees must submit PTO requests through the HR Portal at least 5 business days in advance for planned absences. Requests for 5 or more consecutive days require manager approval at least 2 weeks in advance to allow for workload planning.

Unplanned absences due to illness may be reported the same day via the HR Portal or by notifying a manager directly, with retroactive entry into the system.

## Carryover and Payout
Up to 5 unused PTO days may be carried over into the following calendar year. Any balance beyond 5 days is forfeited on December 31 unless the employee's state of residence legally requires payout. Upon voluntary or involuntary termination, unused accrued PTO is paid out at the employee's current base rate, in accordance with local law.

## Blackout Periods
Certain teams (notably Customer Support and Release Engineering) may have blackout periods during major product launches or end-of-quarter close, during which PTO requests require additional director-level approval.
