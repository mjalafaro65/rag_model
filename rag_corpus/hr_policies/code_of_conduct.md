# Code of Conduct
**Northlane Systems — Human Resources**
**Effective Date:** June 1, 2024
**Document ID:** HR-POL-001

## Purpose
This Code of Conduct sets expectations for professional behavior across all Northlane Systems employees, contractors, and interns, regardless of location or role.

## Core Principles
1. **Respect** — Treat colleagues, clients, and partners with dignity. Harassment, discrimination, or retaliation of any kind is prohibited.
2. **Integrity** — Represent Northlane and its products honestly in all internal and external communications.
3. **Accountability** — Take ownership of mistakes and participate constructively in post-incident reviews.
4. **Confidentiality** — Protect proprietary information, customer data, and trade secrets in accordance with the Data Privacy Policy (LEGAL-POL-003).

## Anti-Harassment and Non-Discrimination
Northlane prohibits discrimination or harassment based on race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, sexual orientation, gender identity, or any other protected characteristic under applicable law. Employees who witness or experience violations should report them to HR or through the anonymous Ethics Hotline.

## Conflicts of Interest
Employees must disclose any outside employment, financial interest, or personal relationship that could reasonably create a conflict with Northlane's business interests. Disclosures are made via the annual Conflict of Interest form or as situations arise.

## Gifts and Business Courtesies
Employees may accept gifts from vendors or partners valued under $75. Gifts exceeding this threshold must be reported to a manager and may need to be declined or donated, depending on circumstances.

## Use of Company Resources
Company equipment, software licenses, and paid tools are provided for business use. Incidental personal use is permitted provided it does not interfere with job performance or violate the Acceptable Use Policy (IT-SEC-002).

## Reporting Violations
Violations of this Code may be reported to a direct manager, HR Business Partner, or anonymously through the Ethics Hotline at ethics.northlane.example. Retaliation against employees who report in good faith is strictly prohibited and may itself be grounds for termination.

## Enforcement
Violations of this Code may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination, depending on severity, as determined by HR in consultation with the employee's management chain.
