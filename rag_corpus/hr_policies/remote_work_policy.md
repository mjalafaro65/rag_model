# Remote Work Policy
**Northlane Systems — Human Resources**
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026
**Document ID:** HR-POL-014

## Purpose
This policy defines the terms under which Northlane Systems employees may work remotely, either on a full-time or hybrid basis.

## Eligibility
Employees are eligible for remote work arrangements if they:
- Have completed at least 90 days of employment
- Hold a role classified as "remote-eligible" by their department head
- Have no active performance improvement plan (PIP)

Roles requiring physical presence (lab technicians, hardware QA, facilities staff) are not eligible for remote arrangements.

## Work Schedule Expectations
Remote employees are expected to be available during core hours of 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in their assigned time zone. Outside of core hours, employees may adjust their schedule with manager approval, provided total weekly hours meet the standard 40-hour requirement.

## Equipment and Reimbursement
Northlane provides a one-time home office stipend of $500 for approved remote employees, covering items such as monitors, chairs, and desks. Ongoing internet costs are reimbursed up to $50/month upon submission of a receipt through the Expense Portal.

## Security Requirements
Remote employees must:
- Connect to company systems only via the Northlane VPN
- Keep company laptops updated with the latest security patches within 5 business days of release
- Avoid working from public, unsecured Wi-Fi networks without VPN active
- Store no company data on personal, unmanaged devices

Failure to comply with these requirements may result in revocation of remote work privileges. See the Acceptable Use Policy (IT-SEC-002) for additional detail on device security.

## Hybrid Schedule Requirements
Employees on a hybrid arrangement must be in the office a minimum of 2 days per week, as coordinated with their team. Office attendance is tracked via badge swipe and reviewed quarterly by department heads.

## Review and Revocation
Remote work arrangements are reviewed annually alongside performance reviews. Northlane reserves the right to revoke remote work privileges at its discretion, with 30 days' written notice, in cases of declining performance, role changes, or business need.
