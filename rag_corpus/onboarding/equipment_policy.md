# Equipment Policy
**Northlane Systems — IT & People Operations**
**Effective Date:** 2025
**Document ID:** ONB-GDE-004

## Standard Equipment
All new employees receive a company-owned laptop (MacBook Pro or ThinkPad, based on role and team standard), a monitor, keyboard, and mouse for office or home use. Engineering roles additionally receive access to cloud development environments provisioned by the Platform team.

## Requesting Additional Equipment
Additional equipment (a second monitor, ergonomic accessories, specialized hardware for testing) can be requested through the IT Portal. Requests under $200 are auto-approved; requests above that threshold require manager sign-off.

## Loaner Equipment
Employees traveling internationally or requiring temporary replacement equipment (e.g., while a laptop is being repaired) may request loaner equipment from the IT Help Desk with at least 3 business days' notice where possible.

## Equipment Return
All company equipment must be returned to IT within 5 business days of an employee's last day of employment, or shipped back using a prepaid label provided by IT for remote employees. Unreturned equipment may be charged to the employee's final paycheck where permitted by local law.

## Security Requirements for Equipment
All company laptops are enrolled in MDM and encrypted by default (FileVault or BitLocker). See the Acceptable Use Policy (IT-SEC-002) for full requirements on device management and remote access. Employees may not disable disk encryption or remove security agents under any circumstances.

## Personal Devices
Personal devices (BYOD) may access email and Slack via approved mobile apps only, subject to a minimal MDM profile enforcing screen lock and remote wipe capability. Personal devices may not access source code repositories, production systems, or restricted customer data under any circumstances.
