# New Hire Handbook
**Northlane Systems — People Operations**
**Effective Date:** 2026 Edition
**Document ID:** ONB-GDE-001

## Welcome
This handbook covers what to expect during your first 30, 60, and 90 days at Northlane Systems.

## Before Day One
IT will provision your laptop, email account, and Slack access 3 business days before your start date. You will receive a welcome email with login instructions and a link to complete new-hire paperwork (I-9, direct deposit, benefits elections) through the HR Portal.

## Day One
- Attend New Hire Orientation (9:00 AM, virtual) covering company history, org structure, and benefits overview
- Complete required compliance training: Code of Conduct (HR-POL-001), Data Privacy basics (LEGAL-POL-003), and Security Awareness
- Meet with your manager to review your 30/60/90 day plan

## First Week
New hires are assigned an onboarding buddy from their team to help navigate tools, processes, and unwritten norms. Expect to complete environment setup, including VPN configuration and access to team-specific systems, by the end of week one.

## First 30 Days
- Complete all assigned onboarding training modules in the Learning Portal
- Meet 1:1 with key cross-functional partners identified in your onboarding plan
- Ship your first small contribution (a documentation fix, a small bug fix, or equivalent for non-engineering roles)

## First 90 Days
Your manager will conduct a 90-day check-in covering ramp progress, feedback on the onboarding process, and goals for the remainder of the year. This is separate from the formal performance review cycle, which begins after 6 months of employment.

## Benefits Enrollment
New hires have 30 days from their start date to enroll in health, dental, and vision benefits through the HR Portal. Missing this window requires waiting until the next open enrollment period or a qualifying life event.
