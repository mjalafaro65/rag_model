# Vendor Management Policy
**Northlane Systems — Legal & Compliance**
**Effective Date:** April 2025
**Document ID:** LEGAL-POL-006

## Purpose
This policy establishes requirements for evaluating, onboarding, and monitoring third-party vendors who provide goods or services to Northlane Systems.

## Vendor Risk Tiers
Vendors are assigned a risk tier at onboarding:
- **Tier 1 (Critical):** Vendors with access to restricted data or critical infrastructure (e.g., cloud hosting, payment processors)
- **Tier 2 (Moderate):** Vendors with access to internal data or non-critical systems
- **Tier 3 (Low):** Vendors with no data access (e.g., office supplies, catering)

## Onboarding Requirements
Tier 1 vendors require:
- A completed security questionnaire reviewed by the Security team
- An executed Data Processing Agreement (DPA), where applicable (see Data Privacy Policy, LEGAL-POL-003)
- Evidence of a current SOC 2 Type II report or equivalent, reviewed annually
- Legal review and signature on the Master Services Agreement (MSA)

Tier 2 vendors require a lightweight security questionnaire and standard MSA terms. Tier 3 vendors require only standard procurement terms via the Purchasing team.

## Ongoing Monitoring
Tier 1 vendor relationships are reviewed annually, including a refreshed security questionnaire and confirmation of continued SOC 2 compliance. Any material change in a vendor's security posture (e.g., a reported breach) triggers an off-cycle review.

## Vendor Offboarding
When a vendor relationship ends, the requesting department must confirm within 15 business days that:
- All company data has been returned or certified destroyed by the vendor
- All vendor access to Northlane systems has been revoked
- Any recurring billing has been terminated

## Conflicts of Interest
Employees involved in vendor selection must disclose any personal or financial relationship with a prospective vendor, consistent with the Code of Conduct (HR-POL-001).
