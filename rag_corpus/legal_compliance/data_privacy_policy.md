# Data Privacy Policy
**Northlane Systems — Legal & Compliance**
**Effective Date:** May 2025
**Document ID:** LEGAL-POL-003

## Purpose
This policy governs how Northlane Systems collects, processes, stores, and disposes of personal data belonging to employees, customers, and end users, in compliance with applicable data protection laws including GDPR and CCPA.

## Data Classification
Data handled by Northlane is classified into three tiers:
- **Public:** Marketing materials, published documentation
- **Internal:** Business plans, internal metrics, non-sensitive employee data
- **Restricted:** Customer personal data, payment information, health data, authentication credentials

Restricted data requires encryption at rest and in transit, and access is limited to employees with a documented business need.

## Data Minimization
Teams collecting personal data must limit collection to what is strictly necessary for the stated purpose. New data collection initiatives involving customer data require a Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) reviewed by Legal before launch.

## Retention and Deletion
Customer personal data is retained only as long as necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected, or as required by law. Standard retention periods:
- Active customer account data: retained for duration of contract plus 90 days
- Support ticket data: 3 years
- Billing records: 7 years (regulatory requirement)

Upon a verified deletion request (see Data Subject Rights below), personal data must be deleted or anonymized within 30 days across all production and backup systems.

## Data Subject Rights
Individuals may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal data. Requests are submitted via privacy@northlane.example and must be fulfilled within 30 days under GDPR, or 45 days under CCPA, unless an extension is legally justified and communicated to the requester.

## Third-Party Data Sharing
Personal data may only be shared with third-party vendors under an executed Data Processing Agreement (DPA). Legal must review and approve any new vendor relationship involving restricted data before data is transferred.

## Breach Notification
In the event of a confirmed data breach involving personal data, Legal and Security jointly assess notification obligations. See the Security Incident Response Policy (IT-SEC-009) for the operational incident process.
