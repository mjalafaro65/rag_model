# Acceptable Use Policy
**Northlane Systems — IT & Security**
**Effective Date:** September 1, 2025
**Document ID:** IT-SEC-002

## Scope
This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and interns who use Northlane-owned devices, networks, or cloud accounts.

## Acceptable Use
Company systems are provided for business purposes. Limited personal use (checking personal email, casual browsing) is permitted as long as it does not:
- Consume excessive bandwidth or storage
- Violate any local, state, or federal law
- Expose the company to security or reputational risk

## Prohibited Activities
The following are strictly prohibited on any Northlane device or network:
- Installing unauthorized or unlicensed software
- Disabling or bypassing endpoint security agents (antivirus, EDR)
- Connecting personal storage devices (USB drives) without prior IT approval
- Accessing, storing, or transmitting illegal content
- Using company accounts to access competitor systems in an unauthorized manner

## Device Management
All company laptops are enrolled in Mobile Device Management (MDM) and must remain enrolled at all times. Employees may not factory-reset, jailbreak, or otherwise remove management profiles from company devices.

## Password and Authentication Requirements
All accounts must use multi-factor authentication (MFA) where available. See the Password Policy (IT-SEC-005) for detailed password composition and rotation requirements.

## Remote Access
Remote access to internal systems must go through the corporate VPN or an approved Zero Trust access gateway. Direct exposure of internal services to the public internet is prohibited without Security team sign-off. See also the Remote Work Policy (HR-POL-014) for related expectations on remote employees.

## Monitoring
Northlane reserves the right to monitor network traffic, email, and device activity for security and compliance purposes, consistent with applicable law. Employees should have no expectation of privacy when using company systems.

## Consequences of Violation
Violations may result in suspension of system access, disciplinary action, or termination, depending on severity and intent.

## Policy Owner
Chief Information Security Officer (CISO), Northlane Systems
